# Paperclip Contract

## Provisional status

Phase 9 is locally validated but lacks committed live-node certification. This
Stage 0 contract is provisional planning only. Paperclip is not installed or
enabled, and this document grants it no execution authority.

Paperclip supplies companies, employees, projects, issues, assignments, comments,
heartbeats, sessions, transcripts, skills, worktrees, checkpoints, and cost/usage
observations. The Hermes adapter maps them to governed identities and immutable
jobs.

Paperclip assignment or comment-triggered wake does not admit execution. Hermes
validates role, worktree, tools, network, secrets, budget, approval, evidence,
deadline, retry, and cancellation policy. Cost reports do not expand Hermes
budgets. Duplicate job ownership is rejected.
