# Self-Evolution Policy

## Provisional status

Phase 9 is locally validated but lacks committed live-node certification. This
Stage 0 policy is provisional planning only. Self-Evolution is not installed or
enabled, and this document grants it no execution or promotion authority.

Initial scope is low-risk skills only. Traces are redacted before curated dataset
creation. Candidates require fixed budgets, immutable dataset/model/cost/change
records, constraints, baseline comparison, independent regression review, a
candidate PR, shadow and canary evaluation, explicit approval, and preserved
rollback target.

The generator cannot be the sole evaluator. Governance, credentials, financial
permissions, deployment authority, protected branches, automatic merge, and
continuous automated evolution are excluded.
